This release rebuilds the trading record. The first start after updating upgrades the record to
schema 29, after copying it into `backups/` beside it, and takes a few seconds on a large record.
A build older than 0.2.0 refuses to open the upgraded record and says so. To go back, reinstall the
older build and restore the copy from `backups/`.

Inside the record there is now one table per thing. Orders from every venue and both sides share one
table with a satellite per venue; every piece of work in progress is the hops of a command; what other
sources say about an asset is one evidence table; per-surface bookkeeping has one table and no
triggers; the ledger no longer names holdings. Fifteen tables, thirty-nine triggers and the walk
telemetry are gone, the record keeps planner statistics, and observations nothing cites are swept
after ninety days.

**Prices and the portfolio.** The portfolio values what you hold at market prices, keeps a daily
snapshot of that value, and splits it by category. A CSFloat wallet counts among your holdings, with
what you can spend and what is still pending shown apart, and money is grouped by the currency it is
held in. *Settings › Currency* picks the currency figures are shown in, and each account's card
shows its Steam wallet.

**Items.** A search bar finds any item, and each item has a page: its price at the top, a price
chart, and the float curve across every wear, on Steam and on CSFloat.

**Filtering.** The filter bar suggests as you type: `f` offers a float bound, `fact` offers Factory
New, and item names come back ordered by how many of each you hold. `/` or Ctrl+F reaches it from
anywhere on the page, and it works from the keyboard alone. Filtering by a large category, such as
every sticker, across several accounts no longer fails.

**Storage units.** Moving hundreds of items into or out of a storage unit now goes at the pace the
Game Coordinator accepts, and the work panel counts the move as it goes, with a progress bar and the
reason for any failures shown once.

**Delists and buy-order cancels are sent again.** When a later walk of the market proves one did not
land, the app sends it again, up to three more times, and the work panel shows it waiting to retry.

**Unexplained orders can be closed.** *Records › Orders* lets you close an order the app cannot
account for as cancelled or as sold. Your answer is kept beside what the app observed, which it
never rewrites.

**CSlytics account, optional.** *Settings › CSlytics account* signs you in to cslytics.com through your
browser. It unlocks nothing yet, and the app works the same without it.
