Prices now need a free CSlytics account and the portfolio adds a float value, while long trade
histories and transfers from storage now finish.

- **CSlytics account.** Prices, order books, float curves and the Items and Portfolio pages need a
  free CSlytics sign-in; listing, buy orders, transfers and storage units work without one.
- **Signing in.** The bottom left shows whether you are signed in, each launch offers sign-in until
  you tick Don't show this again, and approving it leaves your browser on cslytics.com.
- **Portfolio prices.** Signed in, the portfolio is priced at once from CSlytics' hourly snapshot of
  the Steam market, and only items it does not cover wait for their own order book.
- **Float prices.** The portfolio shows a float value beside the market value, each skin at its own
  float, selecting a skin shows its CSFloat float price, and staged listings are marked on its curve.
- **Trade history.** An account with a long, busy trade history now reads every trade instead of
  starting over, and a history read no longer holds up a listing or a refresh.
- **Phone confirmations.** Listing or repricing shows "waiting for your phone confirmation" in the
  work panel while Steam waits, instead of going straight from sent to done.
- **Transfers from storage.** A transfer to another of your accounts takes every item out of storage
  in one go and sends one offer, and cases or stickers complete as soon as they arrive.
- **Filters.** Location, category and Tradable are typed in the search bar, and Tradable now shows
  every unlocked item, storage included, even on an account not traded from yet.
- **Currencies.** Wallets in Swedish kronor, Argentine pesos or Belarusian rubles now show their
  currency code on Listings, Buy orders and the work panel, as they already did on Portfolio.
- **What's new.** *Settings › General › About* has a What's new button that opens these notes on
  cslytics.com.

**Before you update.** From this version, prices and the Items and Portfolio pages need a free
CSlytics account. The app offers sign-in when it starts, and you can sign in later from the bottom
left of the window.
