Steam's November changes are handled, with the wallet reading again and prices changed on the
listing itself, and Items can now be browsed by weapon, type and collection.

- **Steam wallet.** Steam changed its market page on 23 November and wallet balances stopped
  updating; they read again.
- **Price changes on Steam.** Reprice now changes the price on the listing itself, so it never
  leaves the market; a higher price applies at once and a lower one after you confirm it.
- **Price-change cooldown.** Steam allows one change per listing every five minutes; a listing
  counts down on its tile, and a change sent early is held and goes out when the time is up.
- **Steam fees.** The minimum fee is now read from your own wallet, so a cheap listing shows what
  its buyer really pays; Australian-dollar wallets moved to 2c in November.
- **Storage units.** Renaming a storage unit works again; Counter-Strike changed how a rename is
  sent in October and earlier versions could no longer do it.
- **CSFloat.** An item lists as soon as it is in your inventory, retrying for ten minutes while
  CSFloat catches up, and a reprice is done as soon as CSFloat answers with the new price.
- **Items.** Typing a weapon, a type, a collection or a rarity on Items shows its skins as tiles,
  and the page opens on every collection, the newest first.
- **Listed items.** An item on sale wears a tag for each marketplace in Inventory, its Reprice opens
  the listing in the listing workspace, and Go to listing takes you to it.
- **Stickers and charms.** Hovering an applied sticker or charm shows its Steam price, the inspector
  estimates what they add to the item, and the search bar finds items by what is applied.
- **Time zone.** *Settings › General* has a Time zone choice, and every date and time in the window
  follows it.

**Before you update.** The first start upgrades your trading record, after copying it into
`backups/` beside it; 0.2.3 cannot open the upgraded record, and going back means reinstalling it
and restoring the copy. A lower price on a Steam listing now waits for your confirmation, and the
listing stays on sale at the price it has until then.

**For developers.** The engine's `REPRICE` command now edits a Steam listing's price in place; the
cancel and relist it used to be is `RELIST`. Steam fee terms take the minimum a wallet read learned
(`FeeTerms.walletMinimumCents`). The trading record is at schema version 36.
